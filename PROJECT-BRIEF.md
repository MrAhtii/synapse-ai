# 🧠 Synapse AI
### Intelligent Learning, Powered by AI

> Turn your study material into a personalized learning system.

## 🏆 Project Snapshot
- Team: Synapse Syndicate
- Developer: Solo Developer
- Built with: Native Builder
- AI: Google Gemini
- Frontend: React + TypeScript
- Backend: Supabase
- Deployment: Vercel

## 💡 The Problem
Students have notes, PDFs, and lectures scattered across different places,
but turning them into an effective study plan takes time.

## 🚀 Our Solution
Synapse AI transforms study materials into:
- 📄 Smart Summaries
- 🧠 AI Flashcards
- 📝 AI Quizzes
- 🎯 Daily Missions
- 📊 Learning Analytics
- 🔥 XP & Learning Streaks
- 💪 Strong & Weak Topic Detection

## 🔄 How It Works

Study Material
      ↓
PDF Upload
      ↓
AI Processing
      ↓
Summary + Flashcards + Quiz
      ↓
Student Learning Activity
      ↓
Analytics + XP + Personalized Insights

## ✨ What Makes Synapse AI Different

Instead of simply generating content, Synapse AI creates a
continuous learning loop:

**Learn → Practice → Analyze → Improve → Repeat**

The platform combines generative AI with gamification and
learning analytics to make studying more personalized and engaging.
